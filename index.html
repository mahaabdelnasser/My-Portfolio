About Me

The marketer behind the campaign

Digital Marketing Specialist — Trainee at DEPI

Every brand has a story worth telling. Mine started with a blank canvas and one nagging question: why do some brands get remembered while others just get scrolled past?

That question pulled me into digital marketing. I started from the visual side — Photoshop and Illustrator — learning how to make someone feel something in three seconds flat. Then I went deeper: audience targeting, content marketing, SEO, and paid advertising through the Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative (DEPI) by MCIT.

Alongside that, I'm studying Marketing at Thebes Academy (2024 – 2028), where I learned how to build a marketing plan, understand target audiences and think in strategy — not just in posts.

Today I don't just make things look good. I build campaigns that reach the right person, at the right moment, with the right message — and I measure them, learn from them, and make them better.

What makes me different

I'm a hybrid: a designer's eye with a marketer's brain. Too many campaigns are pretty but pointless, or strategic but ugly. I deliver both — visuals that stop the scroll, and a strategy behind every single one of them.
